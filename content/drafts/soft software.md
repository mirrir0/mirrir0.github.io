i grew up during the quirky age of connection. By all accounts, the  mileu, the technologies, and the trendy companies dominating the internet in the 2010s fundamentally valued the understanding that the building of technology was the designing of the moment. Not in some mechanized way, but in the way that writing code and participating in a world where distance was measured in milliseconds was more about writing and less about the (programming) language you were writing in.

I become an adult during naive age of the internet. Anonymous was a social meme that scared people in suits. And  between the arab spring and the defeat of ISIS I built my first computer, wrote my first lines of python, and developed the larval understandings of the digital world and its consequences. In highschool I learned from Michael Nielsen the future that we live in from his RNN tutorials. 

I saw Rails build an entire world that Microsoft is killing a part of.
