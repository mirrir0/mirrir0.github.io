Making predictions is easy, but being able to articulate them well enough such that they can be falsified and compared against is really worthwhile. For that reason, I want to write mine down for the year and see where things fall in January 2027.

# 2026 Predictions

## 1. small LLMs will make consumer ai **Not Suck**

- LLM powered products suck for the general consumers right now
  - this is not really consumer's problem
  - in general, B2B software is both easier to fund and easier to sell
  - the architecture of how AI is deployed and developed does not incline itself to people just using it. they end up needing to do potentially dangerous and insecure things with their data and their computers

## 2. small interpersonal systems, not platforms

- users in general don't really want platforms. They are a thing that has been effectively built to encircle them and their markets.
  - some comment about the commons
- building bridges is a much more effective and important things for technologists to be working on that tryingto deepen the waters across islands
